FAQ

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Rosterly works offline with no sign-up: open the app and start.

Do I lose my data if I change phone?

No: create a backup in the app and save it to Drive or iCloud, then restore it on the new phone in seconds. The phone’s own automatic backup also includes the app’s data.

Can I issue receipts?

Yes, with Pro you create numbered receipts and a yearly payment statement for each athlete, with your club’s letterhead.

Which languages are available?

English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch and Polish, with your local currency.

What does lifetime Pro include?

Every Pro feature forever, with a single payment and all future updates. No subscription to remember.

Is there a free app to manage a sports team?

Yes: Rosterly is free for up to 10 athletes and brings attendance, monthly fees, medical certificates and WhatsApp reminders together in one app for coaches and clubs, with no account and your data on your phone.

How do I track monthly player fees without a spreadsheet?

In Rosterly you mark a fee as paid in one tap: the month screen shows collected vs outstanding, arrears are worked out for you and anyone who hasn't paid gets a ready-written WhatsApp reminder.

How can I keep track of athletes' medical certificate expiry dates?

In Rosterly you snap the certificate and set the expiry date: the app alerts you 30 and 7 days before, and at roll call you see straight away who isn't covered, with a WhatsApp nudge to the parent one tap away.
